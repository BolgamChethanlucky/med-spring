About Us

The Apothecary Index is designed to provide clear, reliable, and beautifully organized insights into common health complaints, herbal care, and when it is essential to seek professional medical intervention.

Self-care

Common, self-limiting complaints. Home care and general OTC categories are usually enough.

PHYSICIAN

See a doctor

Needs an actual diagnosis or prescription. We describe how it's managed — never a dose.

EMERGENCY

Act now

Call emergency services immediately. No home remedy belongs on this card.
